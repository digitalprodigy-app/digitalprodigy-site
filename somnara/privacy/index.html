Privacy Policy

Somnara: Dream Interpreter · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Somnara is built by Digital Prodigy LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: your dream journal stays on your device and we never see your dreams. If you subscribe, we keep a small, pseudonymous record of that subscription so we can tell which campaigns bring in subscribers.

What we collect

Somnara has no user accounts, no registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or your Apple ID, and we hold no database of users. The only data we keep is the subscription measurement described below, which is linked to a random identifier and not to your identity.

Your dream journal stays on your device

Every dream you record, along with its interpretation, symbols, emotions and dates, is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage. This data is never uploaded to us and is not backed up to any server we control. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. You can also erase everything at any time from Settings.

How dream interpretation works

To interpret a dream, the app sends the text of that single dream to our server, a small relay hosted by Cloudflare, which adds our access key and forwards it to the Anthropic API, which runs the AI model that produces the interpretation. This is the only situation in which information leaves your device, and it happens only when an interpretation is requested.

  • Only the text of the individual dream is sent. No identifier, no account, no other dreams and nothing else from your journal.
  • The interpretation is returned to your device and stored there.
  • Our relay passes the text and the interpretation straight through: it does not store or log them, and no one at Digital Prodigy reads them. Cloudflare keeps basic technical records of each request (such as the time, the result and network information, never the dream or the interpretation) for a few days.

Anthropic processes this text as our service provider in order to generate the interpretation. Their handling of API data is described in their privacy policy.

Voice recording

If you choose to record a dream by speaking, the app uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process this recognition on device or on Apple's servers, as described in Apple's own privacy documentation. We do not receive or store any audio, and the recording is not kept after transcription.

Subscriptions

Somnara Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles the entire purchase and processes your payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Subscription and campaign measurement

To understand which of our campaigns bring in subscribers, we keep a minimal record of each subscription:

  • When you start a subscription, the app sends our server a random identifier that the app creates on your device (it is not your name, Apple ID or device identifier), the plan you chose and, if you installed Somnara from an Apple Search Ads ad, an attribution token provided by Apple's AdServices framework.
  • We send that token to Apple, which tells us the campaign, ad group, keyword and country of the ad, or that the install did not come from an ad.
  • Apple sends our server notifications about that subscription (for example when a free trial starts, renews, is cancelled, expires or is refunded), with the plan, dates, transaction numbers and the same random identifier.

We use this only to count free trials, payments and cancellations by source and campaign. We do not use it to track you across apps or websites, we do not combine it with data from other companies, and we do not sell or share it. AdServices attribution does not require tracking permission. The data is stored on our server provider, Cloudflare, and kept only as long as we need it for these reports.

Analytics and advertising

Somnara contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party trackers of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites. The only advertising-related data we use is Apple's own attribution for Apple Search Ads, described above.

Children

Somnara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Your journal never leaves your device, so you have full and direct control of it: you can export it or erase it entirely from within the app at any time. The subscription measurement is pseudonymous and we cannot link it to you on our own; if you would like it deleted, write to us and we will help you find and remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
