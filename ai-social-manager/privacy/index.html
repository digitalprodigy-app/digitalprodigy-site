Privacy Policy

AI Social Manager · Last updated: 8 October 2026

AI Social Manager is a private, internal tool built and operated by Digital Prodigy LLC to plan, create, publish and measure social media content for our own apps. It is not offered to the public: its only user is its operator, and it publishes only to accounts we own. This policy explains what information the tool accesses and how it is handled.

Information from Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. With the authorization of the channel owner, it accesses:

  • Basic information about our YouTube channel: channel ID, title, handle and uploads playlist.
  • The list of videos we upload, and their public statistics (views, likes and comments).
  • Permission to upload videos to our channel. A video is uploaded only when the operator clicks “Publish to YouTube”.

Without authorization, it also reads public YouTube data (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, durations and public statistics of public Shorts and channels) to research which kinds of content perform well in our niche.

Information from other services

  • TikTok: to send drafts to our own account's inbox and to read our own posts' statistics.
  • App Store Connect: our apps' aggregate download reports.

How we use it

  • To publish our own videos, and to detect and measure their performance on our private dashboard.
  • To learn which formats, hooks and topics work best for our own content.
  • Public data about other creators' videos is used only internally, to inform our own content. It is never published or shared.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except with the service providers below and only as needed to run the tool.

Service providers

  • Hetzner (Germany) hosts the server and database.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that helps write our own content and analyse public video metadata (such as titles and thumbnails) for our research. Their handling of API data is described in their privacy policy.
  • ElevenLabs turns our own scripts into voice-over. No Google user data is sent to it.

Storage, security and retention

  • Data is stored on our private server, which is reachable only through a private network.
  • Access tokens for connected accounts are encrypted at rest.
  • Statistics of our own videos are kept on our dashboard to measure our own results.
  • Public data about other creators' videos and channels is deleted within 30 days.

Your choices

  • The YouTube channel can be disconnected in the tool at any time, which deletes its stored access tokens.
  • Access can also be revoked from your Google security settings.
  • On request, we delete stored Google user data within 7 days.

YouTube and Google

By using the YouTube features of the tool, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data Google processes.

Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

The tool uses a single session cookie to keep its operator signed in. It uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
